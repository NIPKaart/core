# Locales

## Name a parking space the same way everywhere
Dutch: "gehandicaptenparkeerplaats" is the official term (sign E6); use it in introductions and explanations. "parkeerplaats" is the short form in the interface for community and municipal places alike ("Is de parkeerplaats er nog?", "Parkeerplaats toevoegen"). Keep "plek" for a location or address only ("Zoek een adres of plek"), never for the parking space itself. Use "parkeervak" only for the painted bay; a municipal record can describe several bays. Do not use "parkeerplek" or "invalide". English: "parking space" for the parking space, "place" only for a location.
